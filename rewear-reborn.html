A Note from the Founder

Closet full of 'nothing to wear', we say without a care,
while silent clothes keep asking us, 'will you ever really share?'
Dresses on the hanger, lying flat on the floor,
not broken, not useless… they just deserve more.

Fashion isn't dead when a trend feels worn,
it's waiting for a second life; ReWear & ReBorn.
ReWear is simple: love it again, keep it in the light,
ReBorn is deeper: pass it on, make the story right.

Every piece we rescue is less fabric turned to waste,
less weight on the planet, more beauty re-placed.
This isn't just shopping, it's choosing how we live,
a little less we throw away, a little more we give.

This is a beginning — a small step, not a storm,
a way to walk forward, and gently change the norm.
When we move together, one choice, one dress at a time,
hearts, habits and closets all start to realign.

I'm Uzma. This is ReWear & ReBorn.
If your clothes still have a heartbeat, what will you let them be reborn as…
instead of just staying worn?
U
UzmaFounder · ReWear & ReBorn

About Us

Clothes That Come Back to Life

ReWear & ReBorn was started by Uzma with a simple belief — every great piece of clothing deserves a second chance. We hand-pick preloved summer, winter, western styles, shoes and accessories so they can travel from one home to another, loved all over again.

Every item is carefully inspected before listing. We only sell what we'd wear ourselves.
